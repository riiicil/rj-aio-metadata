    - Removed redundant second click on `cardElement`. `selectCard()` now clicks the innermost thumbnail target once, allowing natural DOM bubbling to select the item in Vue.
  - **Active Selection Polling in `waitForEditorReady()` (`src/adapters/FreepikAdapter.js`):**
    - Refactored `waitForEditorReady()` to poll until `cardElement` possesses the `selected` class, the sidebar textarea is present, and the sidebar header does not report `Select 0/`.
