### What Was Done
- **Smart Dropup & Viewport Bounds Detection (`src/popup/custom_select.js` & `src/styles/components.css`):**
  - Implemented automatic viewport bounds calculation in `CustomSelect.open()`: checks available space below versus above the trigger.
  - When remaining space below is less than 200px and space above is greater, dynamically flips the dropdown menu to open upwards (`.dropup`) with slide-up animation (`rj-menu-in-up`).
